Buy FAQ

Here is additional information to answer some of the common questions about buying crypto and how to benefit from it with Venga.

Crypto-assets give you access to a global asset class that you can buy in small amounts, at any time. Prices can rise or fall sharply, so only invest money you can afford to lose and take time to understand each asset before you buy.

You can fund your purchase with a transfer from your bank, a bank card or Apple Pay. Fees can differ by method, and you’ll always see them before you confirm an order.

Major cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and Chainlink (LINK), plus a growing list of other assets. The app always shows the current list.

Venga is authorised by Spain’s CNMV as a crypto-asset service provider under MiCA. The app is designed to make buying crypto simple and transparent, with fees shown up front.
